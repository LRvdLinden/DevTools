Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

DevTools is made by PixelForges (Apeldoorn, the Netherlands). We take your privacy seriously: DevTools does not collect, store or share any personal data.

What the app does

  • DevTools connects directly from your device to Apple's official App Store Connect API (api.appstoreconnect.apple.com) using the API key you provide.
  • To show App Store artwork and ratings, the app queries Apple's public iTunes Search API with the Apple IDs of your own apps.
  • There are no servers of ours in between. We have no access to your API key, your apps, your sales data or your reviews.

Data stored on your device

  • Your private key (.p8) is stored only in the Keychain of your device (“this device only”) and is never synced or transmitted elsewhere.
  • Account names, Issuer ID, Key ID, vendor number, settings and cached app/report data are stored locally on your device and shared only with the DevTools widgets and your paired Apple Watch.
  • Deleting an account in the app or deleting the app removes this data.

No tracking

DevTools contains no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs and does not track you across apps or websites.

Children

DevTools is a tool for app developers and is not directed at children.
