DevTools is the companion app for iOS, iPadOS and Apple Watch developers who want to keep an eye on their apps without opening a laptop. Connect your App Store Connect API key once and see every app, every build and every number in one fast, native app.

YOUR APPS AT A GLANCE
• All your apps with their real icons, versions and status
• Clear status badges: Live, Waiting for Review, In Review, Ready for Distribution, Pending Release, Rejected
• "Attention needed" card for rejected versions, apps ready to release and builds missing export compliance
• Instant start with locally cached data, pull to refresh

DOWNLOADS & PROCEEDS
• Installs, new downloads, re-downloads, updates and in-app purchases
• Proceeds per app and for your whole portfolio
• Beautiful interactive charts: scrub through 7 days, 30 days, 90 days or a full year
• Installs per app over time, top countries and devices
• Based on the official Sales and Trends reports from Apple

SHIP NEW VERSIONS FROM ANYWHERE
• Create a new App Store version
• Write What's New, description, keywords, promotional text, support and marketing URLs for every language
• Copy What's New to all languages in one tap
• Edit app name, subtitle and privacy policy URL
• Choose a build, set the release type and submit for App Review
• Cancel a review or release an approved version with one tap

TESTFLIGHT UNDER CONTROL
• All builds grouped by version, with processing and beta status
• Edit What to Test, answer export compliance and expire old builds
• Add builds to test groups, toggle automatic tester notifications and submit for beta review
• Create internal and external groups, enable public links, invite or remove testers

CUSTOMER REVIEWS
• Rating distribution and all reviews with country and date
• Filter by stars or unanswered reviews
• Reply to reviews, edit or delete your replies

WIDGETS & APPLE WATCH
• Downloads widget for one app or all apps, Top apps widget and Review & status widget
• Apple Watch app with overview, your apps and what needs attention
• Watch face complications with downloads, weekly change and apps in review

MULTIPLE ACCOUNTS, PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Add several App Store Connect teams and switch in a tap
• Your private key (.p8) is stored only in the Keychain of your device
• DevTools talks directly to Apple's App Store Connect API — there is no server in between and no data is collected
• Built-in step-by-step guide to create your API key, find your Issuer ID, Key ID and vendor number

Requirements: an Apple Developer Program membership and an App Store Connect API team key. Sales data requires the Admin, Finance or Sales role and your vendor number.
