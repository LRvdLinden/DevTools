App Store Connect brings your iOS, iPadOS, macOS and tvOS apps into Homey. Every app becomes a device that shows its downloads, proceeds, App Store status, live and upcoming version, TestFlight builds and installs, phased release and rating, while an "All apps" device keeps track of your whole portfolio. Use it in Flows to be notified when a version enters App Review, gets approved or goes live, when testers send feedback or when new download figures arrive, and add the dashboard widgets for a quick look at how your apps are doing.

Setup takes a minute: create an App Store Connect API key and add it, together with your vendor number, in the app settings, where a step-by-step guide is included. Your key is stored only on your Homey and talks directly to Apple. This app is not affiliated with Apple Inc.
