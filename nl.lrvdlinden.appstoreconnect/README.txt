App Store Connect brings your iOS, iPadOS, macOS and tvOS apps into Homey.

Add every app from your App Store Connect account as a device and follow:
• Downloads yesterday, last 7 days, last 30 days and all-time
• Updates and proceeds of the last 30 days
• App Store status (Live, Waiting for Review, In Review, Ready for Distribution, Pending Release, Rejected)
• Live version, next version and the latest TestFlight build
• Rating and number of ratings

The "All apps" device shows the totals of your whole portfolio: total downloads, live apps, apps in review and apps that need attention.

Flows
• When: status changed, entered App Review, rejected, ready for distribution, waiting for your release, new live version, new build, build processed, new customer review, rating changed, new download figures, downloads dropped
• And: app is live, in App Review, new version in progress, downloads above a number, rating above a number, live version older than X days
• Then: refresh, release the approved version, reply to a customer review

Dashboard widgets
Downloads (one app or all apps, with an Insights-style chart), Top apps and Review & status – the same design as the DevTools iPhone app.

Setup: create an App Store Connect API team key (Admin role) and paste the Issuer ID, Key ID and the .p8 key in the app settings. Add your vendor number for downloads. A step-by-step guide is included in the settings.

Your API key is stored only on your Homey. This app is not affiliated with Apple Inc.
